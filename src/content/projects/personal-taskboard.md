## The problem

Off-the-shelf task tools didn't fit how I plan my week — especially the mix of project work, step-by-step processes and delivery schedules.

## What I built

- **List** — every task grouped by status, with a smart sort.
- **Board** — Kanban columns with step progress on each card.
- **Calendar** — tasks by due date.
- **Plan** — a timeline built from start dates and working-day estimates.
- **Shipping** — delivery rounds per customer, week by week.

Tasks can carry step-by-step templates, and everything can be exported and imported as a backup file.

## How it works

One HTML file, no install. Data is saved in the browser's local storage. The demo opens with sample tasks — try adding, moving and completing them.
