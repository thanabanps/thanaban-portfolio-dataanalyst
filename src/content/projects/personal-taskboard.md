## The problem

Off-the-shelf task tools didn't fit how I plan my week.

## What I built

A task manager rebuilt through three versions, ending with a dark interface inspired by Asana:

- **List** — every task, sortable.
- **Board** — tasks by status.
- **Calendar** — tasks by due date.
- **Daily** — just today.
