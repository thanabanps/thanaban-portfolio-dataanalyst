## The problem

Management needed one place to check sales by customer and product without asking for a new report each time.

## What I built

- **Monthly YoY bar chart** — this year against last, month by month.
- **Customer ranking table** — key accounts ranked by sales.
- **Product breakdown** — SKU-level view with filters.

## How it works

A Google Sheets data source, prepared from SAP exports, feeds a Looker Studio report shared with the team.
